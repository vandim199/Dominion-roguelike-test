# Coinkeep: Forays (this branch's take)

## Pitch

You hold a Keep with a water wheel that grinds Powder. You march out with a small pack of cards and a row of bastions; every card in your hand is both a move and fuel, so each turn you decide what to play and what to burn. Powder runs left to right, and when a bastion fills it fires. Enemies you can already crush die in a roaring march, the fights near your limit are played by hand, and when you are beaten or wise enough to leave you go home to spend coin. Cards never leave you, shops come rarely, and every card is a small build of its own: levels, milestone jumps and sockets.

## A minute

Press March and watch the enemy that beat you last trip die in one volley among a dozen others. Then a fight at about 3x your need: two turns, one interesting choice. Then a fight at even odds, where you count Powder to make the Mortar fire this turn, and burn a good card to do it because the Archer is about to shoot your back row.

## A session

Twenty to thirty minutes is about five trips of three to five minutes each. Home is thirty seconds of buying up to a milestone, moving a bastion, swapping a card in the pack, reading the scout list of what stands at your wall. A boss every 12 fights hands out a permanent rule change. A caravan every 7 fights offers one card, once.
