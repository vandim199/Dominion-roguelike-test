# Coinkeep: Forays (Claude's take)

## Pitch
You run a Keep that owes everyone money. You go out on short forays, and your hand makes Powder, the board burns it left to right, and each Bastion that fills fires once a turn for a huge number. Energy, hand size, Powder and fill costs stay small forever, so a turn at 1e9 asks the same small question as a turn at 100: which three of these five cards, with that attack coming at that slot? When the road beats you, you go home, spend what you carried, and walk back through the thing that killed you in one shot. Then the road ends you again a few fights further on.
