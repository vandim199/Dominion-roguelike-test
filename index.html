Coinkeep

Build a deck. Pay off the trouble. Hold the town.

How to play

  1. ⚡ Spend Energy. You get 1 ⚡ a turn. Each card shows what it costs to play in its blue pip; Treasures are free. Sparks give ⚡, Scouts draw cards, and the rest give Coin, Buys or strike threats.
  2. 🔨 Merge. Play your Anvil to merge two identical cards from anywhere in your deck into one a level higher — two Coppers make a Silver, two Sparks a Spark II. It costs the same to play and does one more: Silver gives 2 Coin, Spark II 2 ⚡. Copies in your discard pile are used first, then your draw pile, then your hand; the new card goes to your discard pile.
  3. 🛒 Shop the market. It starts with level-one cards; ones that cost more ⚡ to play arrive in later waves. Once you merge a card to a new level, the market starts stocking the level below it. Bought cards go to the bottom of your deck; the two leftmost stalls leave each turn.
  4. 👹 Beat the fuse. Each threat wants something — Coin, Energy, Buys or cards — and has a fuse. Tap its button to pay; one that doesn't want Coin can also be bought off with Coin. If the fuse runs out it detonates: it attacks and costs the town a ❤ (you have 3). Clear one and you may take a free card. Demands climb faster and faster as the waves go on.
  5. 🌱 Grow your cards. Nest Eggs grow Coin every time you play them, Snowballs compound, Saplings sprout every wave, Warrens breed and pair off into higher levels. What a card grows stays on it (🌱), even through merges. A 💔 Cracked card plays two levels higher but may shatter each time you play it; two Cracked twins merge into a stable card.
  6. 🏦 Save in the Vault. Coin you don't spend is saved at the end of the turn and earns interest. Tap the Vault to take it out. Alchemists don't multiply Vault coin.
  7. 🧊 Don't lean on one card. The Snow Queen, and Elites from wave 5, seal your most valuable card in ice until they leave. The Bookworm eats everything your cards would draw until it is full.
  8. 🏰 Hold all three slots. If a threat arrives with every slot full, the town falls.
  9. ✨ Between waves pick a charm that bends the rules, and visit the Inn to trash or copy a card, rest, or merge two different cards into one that does both and costs their average ⚡ (rounded up).
