Coinkeep

Build a deck. Pay off the trouble. Hold the town.

How to play

  1. ⚡ Spend Energy. You get 1 ⚡ a turn. Each card shows what it costs to play in its blue pip; Treasures are free. Sparks give ⚡, Scouts draw cards, and the rest give Coin, Buys or strike threats.
  2. 🔨 Merge. Play your Anvil to merge two identical cards from anywhere in your deck into one a level higher — two Coppers make a Silver, two Sparks a Spark II. It costs the same to play and does one more: Silver gives 2 Coin, Spark II 2 ⚡. Copies in your discard pile are used first, then your draw pile, then your hand; the new card goes to your discard pile.
  3. 🛒 Shop the market. It starts with level-one cards; ones that cost more ⚡ to play arrive in later waves. Once you merge a card to a new level, the market starts stocking the level below it. Bought cards go to the bottom of your deck; the two leftmost stalls leave each turn.
  4. 👹 Beat the fuse. Each threat wants something — Coin, Energy, Buys or cards — and has a fuse. Tap its button to pay. If the fuse runs out it detonates: it attacks and costs the town a ❤. Clear one and you may take a free card, then fuse it straight into one of your cards instead of adding it to your deck. Coin demands climb faster every wave, like antes: a deck that only grows steadily hits a wall.
  5. 💥 Overkill. The Overkill button pours all your Coin into the threats, the most urgent first, then into those still queued: what one doesn't need spills into the next, so a huge turn pays off threats before they arrive. Coin pays for Energy, Buy and card threats too, at a price that grows with the wave. Every threat you clear in a turn makes the rest of that turn's Coin worth ×1.25 (the ⛓ chain). Clear 3 or more in the turn that ends the wave for a 🌊 Wipe: an extra charm or Inn service.
  6. 🗿 Build a Monolith. A fusion's numbers grow ×1.02 for every part beyond the first, compounding, and it costs the average ⚡ of its parts, rounded down. Hermit, Monk, Lone Wolf, Minimalist and Keystone reward a tiny deck. Strikes take at most 40% of what a threat still wants.
  7. 🧊 Don't lean on one card. The Snow Queen, and every Elite, seal your most valuable card in ice until they leave (Warm Mittens: for one turn only). Pay them off in the queue and they never arrive. The Bookworm eats everything your cards would draw until it is full.
  8. 🏰 Hold all three slots. If a threat arrives with every slot full, the town falls.
  9. ✨ Between waves pick a charm that bends the rules, and visit the Inn to trash or copy a card, rest, or merge 2 or 3 different cards into one that does it all. A deck of 6 cards or fewer gets two services. Then 📜 Ante Up if you dare: a contract makes the next wave harder (×2 demands, 2 more threats, or shorter fuses) and pays a rare charm, an extra service or a free fused card if you clear it without losing a heart.
