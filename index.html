Spellkeep

A pocket deckbuilder for wizards. Every spell costs 1 Mana, and the Mana you still hold multiplies your damage.

Classic Coinkeep (22 October)

How to play

Foes come one at a time. Bring each one's health to zero within 5 turns. If one is still standing after its last turn you lose a heart ❤, and with no hearts left the run is over. Each circle is a Foe, an Elite and a Boss; beat the 8th Boss to win, then play on for as long as you last.

Mana ✦ is everything. You start each turn with 3 ✦. Playing any card costs 1 ✦, paid after it resolves. Every hit is multiplied by the ✦ you hold when it lands, so the ✦ you don't spend is your power.

Order matters. A card's lines resolve top to bottom. +3 ✦ then Deal 6 hits with the new ✦; Deal 12 then +3 ✦ hits first and pays later. Gain Mana before you strike, strike with your big spells while ✦ is high, and spend your last ✦ on Arcana. Each card in your hand shows what it would deal right now.

Arcana ◆ is your purse and it keeps between turns and fights. Learn new spells from the Arcane Library at any point in your turn: bought cards go to your discard pile. Every victory pays ◆, more for each turn to spare, so a fast kill is worth it.

Burn is multiplied once, when applied, then hits at the end of every turn for the rest of the fight: cast it early. Runes stay out for the rest of the fight.

After each win, take a card from the spoils. Elites and Bosses offer a relic. After a Boss, visit the Sanctum to purge, empower or copy a card, or rest. Each circle's Boss is shown from its first fight, so you can prepare for its curse.
