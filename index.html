Coinkeep

Build a deck. Pay off the trouble. Hold the town.

How to play

  1. 🃏 Play your hand. Action cards give Cards, Actions, Buys and Coin. Treasures give Coin.
  2. 👹 Beat the fuse. Each threat wants something — Coin, Actions, Buys or cards — and has a fuse. Tap its button to pay. If the fuse runs out it detonates: it attacks, scars one of your cards and costs the town a ❤.
  3. 🧬 Splice your cards. Every threat you clear lets you graft a keyword onto a card in your deck — Encore with +1 Action, a Gold that's Sticky, a Twin Forge. Clear a threat within two turns for a swift bonus.
  4. 🛒 Shop the market. A small set of core cards — splicing is where the depth is. They go to the bottom of your deck. The two leftmost stalls leave each turn. Every wave the stalls stock higher tiers: each tier hits harder and costs more, on the same steep curve as the threats.
  5. 🧊 Don't lean on one card. The Snow Queen, and Elites from wave 5, seal your most valuable card in ice until they leave. The Bookworm eats everything your cards would draw until it is full.
  6. 🏰 Hold all three slots. If a threat arrives with every slot full, the town falls.
  7. ✨ Between waves pick a charm that bends the rules, and visit the Inn to upgrade, copy, mend or trash a card, or rest.

Classic mode is the original game: a big card pool, and threats that attack every few turns until you pay them off. Cards with ★ are upgraded. Upgrades stack — a Forge★ can turn Gold into Gold★★.
